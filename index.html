Health & Hygiene Awareness

About Objectives Survey Activities Gallery Our Team

1. Introduction / Abstract

Health and Hygiene are the foundation of a healthy society. Our CSP project aims to create awareness about personal hygiene, clean surroundings, safe drinking water, balanced diet and disease prevention in our village / town.

Through survey, awareness camps, and cleaning activities, we observed the problems faced by people and suggested simple, practical solutions.

2. Objectives

  • To create awareness about personal and environmental hygiene
  • To educate people about hand washing, dental care, and menstrual hygiene
  • To promote clean drinking water and proper waste disposal
  • To reduce common diseases like cold, fever, dengue, diarrhoea
  • To motivate school children and villagers for daily hygiene habits

3. Survey Details

We conducted a door-to-door survey with 50 families in our village.

TopicObservation
Hand Washing60% people wash hands before eating
Drinking Water70% use filtered water, 30% use direct tap water
Waste DisposalMany throw waste in open places
Toilets90% have toilets, but cleaning is irregular
Health CheckupVery few go for regular checkups

Problems Identified: Lack of awareness, open drainage, plastic waste, unhygienic food habits.

4. Activities Conducted

  • Day 1-2: Survey in village & interaction with people
  • Day 3: Awareness camp in school - Hand wash demo, posters
  • Day 4: Cleaning drive - Village roads, school premises
  • Day 5: Health camp with ASHA worker / PHC - BP, Sugar check
  • Day 6: Plantation & distribution of dustbins, soap, masks

6. Conclusion & Learning

Through this project we understood that small habits like hand washing, drinking clean water, and keeping surroundings clean can prevent 80% of diseases. We learned teamwork, communication, and social responsibility. We will continue to spread health awareness in our society.
